About Us

Ethical Sourcing & Export Excellence

We bridge the gap between local Indonesian growers and global spice demands. Sourced in Blitar, processed with rigorous quality checks, and exported with full transparency.

Spices House Indonesia was established in Blitar, East Java, a volcanic region rich with nutrients perfect for premium spice agriculture. We specialize in sourcing directly from farmers to build sustainable supply lines.

Starting as a local trading house, we expanded to international shipping routes. Today, we support over 500 family farming partners, helping them implement clean harvesting practices that command global premium prices.

Our vision is to become the leading provider of organic and certified Indonesian spices globally, empowering agricultural communities while keeping the traditional flavor heritage alive.

To deliver export-grade cloves, ginger, turmeric, and vanilla with traceabilities. We ensure competitive FOB rates, strict moisture and micro-testing, and worry-free custom clearances.
