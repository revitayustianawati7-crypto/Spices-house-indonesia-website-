Clove Showcase & Process

How We Process Dried Cloves

Our drying method is designed to keep eugenol-rich aroma, stable moisture, and export-grade cleanliness.

Step 1: Selective Harvest

Showcase grade cloves are selected from mature buds and pre-cleaned to remove leaves and stems.

Step 2: Sun Drying

Cloves are spread evenly and turned regularly to achieve target moisture while preserving volatile oil.

Step 3: Final Sorting

Dried buds are graded, cleaned, and packed into export sacks ready for FOB or CIF shipment.

Logistics & Packing

To preserve the delicate volatile oils and prevent moisture absorption during maritime transit, our cloves are packed in double-layered PP bags or strong gunny sacks, tightly containerized, and loaded on desiccated palettes at Tanjung Perak Port, Surabaya.

Accredited Lab Certificates

We provide Certificate of Analysis (COA) for eugenol fractions and moisture, alongside Phytosanitary certification from agricultural inspection offices in Indonesia.
