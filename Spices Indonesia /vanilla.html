Vanilla Beans

Vanilla planifolia

We are a certified exporter of Indonesian vanilla beans. Sourced from optimal microclimate plantations in Java and Bali, our beans are carefully cured over months to build a sweet, deep, and complex vanillin profile. Supple, oily, plump, and packed with aromatic seeds, our vanilla pods are graded to meet the highest gourmet culinary and industrial extract criteria.

Logistics & Packing

To preserve moisture content and protect the complex aromatic compounds during shipping, our vanilla pods are vacuum-packed in heavy-gauge food grade bags (1kg or 5kg packs) and shipped inside insulated shipping cartons via temperature-controlled air freight.

Accredited Lab Certificates

We provide full microbiological profiles, moisture checks, and vanillin analysis (COA) with each batch, alongside standard Phytosanitary documentation.
