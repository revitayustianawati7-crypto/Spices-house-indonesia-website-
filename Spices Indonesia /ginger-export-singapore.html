Revita's Story

How Village Ginger Reached a Singapore Buyer

A practical story about quality upgrades, export readiness, and finding reliable demand.

← Back to Blog

The beginning: After seeing progress in vanilla, Revita turned to ginger. The opportunity was large, but the challenge was familiar: inconsistent farm quality and limited buyer access.

Village-level improvements: She worked with farmers on practical standards: cleaner roots, more consistent sizing, moisture awareness, and better packing flow before shipment.

Finding the first buyer: Revita shared samples and clear specifications with regional prospects. The strongest response came from a Singapore distributor that needed stable supply for wholesale channels.

Order execution: The team prepared lots to match the buyer checklist and completed core export documents: packing list, commercial invoice, COO, and phytosanitary certificate.

Shipping stage: The deal ran under FOB Surabaya, giving the buyer flexibility with forwarding arrangements. Documentation and handover moved smoothly.

Result: The first shipment was accepted and followed by recurring demand. For local farmers, this translated into better price confidence and more predictable sales cycles.

Buyer name is withheld for commercial confidentiality.
