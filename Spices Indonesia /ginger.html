Ginger Types

Indonesian Ginger Types We Supply

Following global export demand, we supply three main Indonesian ginger types for food factories, seasoning brands, and extract manufacturers.

White Ginger (Jahe Putih / Jahe Gajah)

White ginger has a larger rhizome body with a smoother appearance and moderate pungency, making it flexible for many industrial and food-service applications.

Best for: slicing, dehydration, and culinary ingredient production.
View Details Request Quote

Java Ginger (Jahe Emprit)

Java ginger is typically smaller in size but stronger in aroma and heat intensity. It is often chosen by buyers needing concentrated ginger character.

Best for: powder milling, spice blends, and herbal formulations.
View Details Request Quote

Red Ginger (Jahe Merah)

Red ginger is recognized for its deeper skin tone and higher pungency profile. It is widely used in health-oriented products and high-intensity flavor formulas.

Best for: extracts, wellness beverages, and concentrated ingredient products.

Buyer Guide

What Ginger Importers Usually Request

To speed up your quotation, share the following details so we can match the right ginger type and packing format.

1) Type Selection

  • White Ginger / Java Ginger / Red Ginger
  • Whole roots, slices, or powder
  • Target pungency and aroma profile

2) Quality Specs

  • Moisture requirement (max %)
  • Foreign matter tolerance
  • Oil or gingerol preference

3) Packing Plan

  • 25 kg bags, mesh sacks, or custom pack
  • Container target (LCL / 20ft / 40ft)
  • Private label or standard export labeling

4) Trade Terms

  • FOB, CIF, or CFR request
  • Destination port and schedule
  • Required documents: COA, phyto, COO, halal

Logistics & Packing

To preserve freshness and prevent mold during transport, our ginger roots, slices, and powder are packed in moisture-resistant export bags and loaded on clean, desiccated palettes at Tanjung Perak Port, Surabaya.

Accredited Lab Certificates

Every shipment is backed by a Certificate of Analysis (COA) specifying moisture levels and oil extraction ratios, alongside official Indonesian Phytosanitary documentation.

Dry Process

Ginger Drying Process

Our ginger lots are cleaned, sliced, dried, and packed with moisture control for stable export quality.

Process Step Operational Detail

Step 2: Slicing & Drying Ginger is sliced to target thickness, then dried using controlled sun and mechanical methods.
