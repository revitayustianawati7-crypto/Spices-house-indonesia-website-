Our Story

Rooted in the Soil of Blitar, East Java

Spices House Indonesia was founded with a single mission: to provide the highest-grade raw spices grown by local Indonesian smallholders directly to international buyers.

Blitar's unique volcanic soil profile, fed by Mt. Kelud's minerals, is recognized worldwide for producing spices of exceptional potency. Cloves with high eugenol values, ginger with strong aromatic oils, and vanilla pods rich in natural vanillin are cultivated here under generations of agricultural wisdom.

By bypassing multiple layers of local middlemen, we ensure that our farming partners receive fair prices, while foreign clients receive transparent, cost-effective FOB pricing structures.
