Turmeric

Curcuma longa

We supply premium grade turmeric for export from Indonesia. Sourced directly from trusted agricultural partners in Blitar, East Java, our turmeric contains a high curcuminoid index, giving it a rich golden-orange color and intense flavor. Available as turmeric fingers (sliced or whole) and micro-milled powder, our processing ensures low ash levels and strict microbiological control.

Logistics & Packing

To avoid damage or loss of curcumin potency due to humidity, our turmeric is packed in air-tight, multi-layered kraft bags or woven sacks. Containers are lined with thick cardboard and dry gel bags before dispatch from Surabaya Port.

Accredited Lab Certificates

All export lots undergo lab certification (COA) specifying curcumin purity percentage, heavy metals limits, and moisture. Official Indonesian Phytosanitary documentation is attached to all shipments.
